04 · Competition This section is generated by AI

Gonio is filling with tall towers; low-rise at a premium price is almost empty

This whole section was generated by AI from public listings and has not been verified by Flow. Treat prices, heights and positions as indicative.

Where Subtropical wins

  • Low-rise among towers. Only Aurum Fort and Seashell are comparable in height.
  • Villas and townhouses at ~$2.5k/m², against €6k+ for the only branded townhouses nearby.
  • 48 months interest-free versus ~14 months at Next Group and 30 at Green Side.
  • A home, not a hotel room. Almost every rival sells hotel units to investors.

Where it loses

  • Sea distance. Rivals sell 20–50 m and private beaches.
  • No hotel brand to reassure first-time foreign buyers.
  • Price above unbranded towers like Next Gardens and Montemar.
